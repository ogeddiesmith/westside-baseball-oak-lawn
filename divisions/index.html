Divisions

Which division a player lands in, and what changes at each level.

Register a player

Westside uses the PONY division names. A player moves up the ladder as they get older, and the game changes at each rung: who pitches, how big the field is, whether runners can steal.

DivisionAgesWhat it is
ShetlandREPLACE:ageThe first year of organized baseball. Coaches pitch or the ball sits on a tee, everyone bats, everyone fields.
PintoREPLACE:ageCoach pitch into player pitch. The year the game starts to look like baseball.
MustangREPLACE:agePlayer pitch, stealing, and the first full-size version of the game.
BroncoREPLACE:ageA bigger diamond and a faster game.
PonyREPLACE:ageThe oldest house division, and for many players the last year before high school ball.
ColtREPLACE:ageHigh-school-age play. needs board confirmation
PalominoREPLACE:ageThe top of the ladder, for the oldest players in the program. needs board confirmation

Not sure which division? Age on a cutoff date decides it, not school year. Email wbolmain@gmail.com with your child's date of birth and we will tell you exactly where they land.

House and travel are different things

House is the recreational program. Every registered player is placed on a team. Travel is a tryout program with a longer season and more games away from Oak Lawn. Many players do house first and travel later, and plenty do house the whole way.
