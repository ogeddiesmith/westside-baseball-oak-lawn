Sponsoring the club

Westside has been putting Oak Lawn kids on the same fields for decades, run entirely by volunteers. A name on these fields is a different thing from an ad. An ad runs and stops. A sign here is still up when the kid who saw it brings their own children back.

Where the money actually goes

Not into a general account. Sponsorship pays for specific things, and we tell sponsors which one their money paid for.

  • The fields and the location. Drainage, fencing, gates, dugouts, walkways and the concession stand.
  • Equipment. Bats, balls, catching gear, helmets, bases and line markers.
  • Training and year-round programs. Fall ball, winter camps, and coaching resources so a volunteer parent who has never coached can run a practice safely.
  • Registration assistance, so no kid in Oak Lawn misses a season over what it costs.
  • Field maintenance, every week. Infield mix and clay, chalk, drag mats, mowing, aeration, fuel, machine repair, and the parts nobody pictures: portable toilets, trash removal, utilities, supplies and tools. This is what decides whether there is a game on Saturday.
  • Umpires and uniforms, the rest of the recurring cost of fielding a league.

What we are working toward

Next up: lights on the pony field, so evening games are possible and a parent on a day shift can watch their kid play. ADA accessibility, so a wheelchair or a walker gets from the parking area to a seat without help and without crossing grass. Concrete walkways and pads, which is what makes the accessibility real rather than a promise.

After that: batting cage upgrades and pitching machines, concession stand and fencing work, and better travel arrangements for the teams that travel. Running through all of it, less out of pocket for parents.

It goes both ways

We are a few hundred local families and our partners are local businesses. We would rather spend with the people who support us, and where the club needs work done we look at our partner list first. We run partner events through the season so the businesses backing Westside are in the room with the families, rather than being a logo nobody can place.

And you will hear from us during the season, not just before it. We send updates on what your money built, with the project named and the result shown.
