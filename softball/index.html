Register a player

Westside ran girls softball as recently as 2024, and we are rebuilding the program.

Being straight with you about where this stands. We are not taking softball registrations yet, because the age groups, the league and the start date are not set. What we are doing is building a list, so that when it is set we can tell you first and you are not finding out after teams are full.

Register your interest

Email wbolmain@gmail.com with your daughter's age and your town. That is all we need. No payment, no commitment.

Why we are rebuilding it

Because there are girls in Oak Lawn who want to play and the club has run this before. If you have a daughter who wants to play, the most useful thing you can do is get on the list, and the second most useful is tell another parent.
