Westside Warriors travel baseball

A longer season and a higher level, for players who want it.

Register a player

The Westside Warriors are the club's travel program: a longer season, a higher level, and games away from Oak Lawn.

What happens if my kid does not make a team

They play house baseball at Westside. Trying out for travel does not put a player's season at risk, and we would rather say that plainly than have a family skip the tryout because they are afraid of what a cut would mean.
