House baseball

The recreational program. No tryouts, no cuts.

Register a player

Every registered player is placed on a team

That is the whole promise of house baseball, and it is the reason most families start here. There is no tryout, no cut, and no bench-warming as a season-long condition.

What house asks of you

Practices and games during the week and at weekends, at the Westside fields. Volunteer help is what makes the club work: coaches, team parents, concessions and field crew are all parents.
