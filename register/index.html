Register a player

How registration works

  1. Create or sign in to a parent account on Stack Sports, the club's registration system.
  2. Add your player and choose a division.
  3. Pay, and you are done. Team placement follows once registration closes.

Go to registration

Registration is hosted on Stack Sports and opens in a new window. If you have registered a Westside player before, use the same login rather than creating a second account, or your player history will be split across two.
