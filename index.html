Every kid plays.

Westside is a volunteer-run baseball organization in Oak Lawn. We run a house league, where every registered player is placed on a team, and a travel program, the Westside Warriors, for players who want a longer season and a higher level.

What parents ask first

Will my kid actually play?

In house, yes. Every registered player is placed on a team. Travel is a tryout program, and the honest answer to what happens if a player is not selected is on the travel page.

Do we have to live in Oak Lawn?

The club's published rules state there are no identified boundaries. More in the FAQ.

What does it cost?

Fees are set by the board each season. Current fees and dates.

Girls softball is coming back

Westside fielded girls softball as recently as 2024. We are rebuilding it. Register your interest and we will contact you as soon as the season and age groups are set.
